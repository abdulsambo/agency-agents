How the difficulty adapts

After every game your rating moves up or down based on the result and how strong the opponent was (the Elo system that chess federations use). Beat a stronger level and you gain more; lose to a weaker one and you drop more.

In adaptive mode the next opponent is picked a little above your rating, so most games feel winnable but stretch you. Lower levels search fewer moves ahead and sometimes miss captures or make random slips, the way beginners do. Higher levels look deeper and stop making mistakes.
